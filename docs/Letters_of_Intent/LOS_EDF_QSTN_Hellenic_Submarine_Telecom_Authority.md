**Subject: Commitment and Institutional Support for AETERNA-QSTN Sovereign Quantum Tactical Network Proposal**

Dear Members of the Evaluation Panel,

We, the undersigned representative organization, hereby express our **unconditional support** and **firm commitment to participate** in the proposal **AETERNA-QSTN (Sovereign Quantum Tactical Network)**, submitted under the EDF-2026-RA "CYBER-QSTN" call by the coordinating applicant **AETERNA** (PIC: `865986222`).

As a critical stakeholder in European digital sovereignty and military infrastructure resilience, we recognize the urgent necessity of securing submarine fiber-optic trunks from physical sabotage, kinetic anchor dragging, and quantum cryptographic harvest attacks. The AETERNA-QSTN project introduces a highly innovative defense approach by retrofitting existing communication channels with non-intrusive coherent Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS), State of Polarization (SOP) monitoring, lattice-based Post-Quantum Cryptography (ML-KEM-1024 / QKD), and sub-millisecond eBPF kernel apoptosis isolation.

#### Our Commitment to the Project:
1. **Landing Station & QKD Integration:** We commit to providing secure access to our landing terminals and subsea defense trunks for the installation of quantum key distribution (QKD) and optical monitoring equipment (WP1).
2. **Tactical Threat Signature Calibration:** We will collaborate in calibrating the real-time Mojo SIMD AI signal separators to classify submarine threats, vessel movements, and physical anchor drag anomalies along Mediterranean and Black Sea paths (WP2).
3. **eBPF Apoptosis Validation:** We agree to validate the eBPF Sentinel process apoptosis loops at our landing station interfaces to ensure immediate (<1ms) traffic rerouting during simulated physical security breaches (WP3).
4. **Resources & Institutional Alignment:** We confirm our participation in accordance with the 100% EU Grant rules of the European Defence Fund.

We are fully convinced that the expertise of **AETERNA** in sovereign, zero-entropy software architectures, combined with our operational landing infrastructure, makes AETERNA-QSTN an exemplary defense project that will materially enhance the security and sovereignty of the European Union.
