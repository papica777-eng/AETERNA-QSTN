◆ System Architecture

Defence-in-Depth Signal Pipeline

A four-layer sovereign pipeline from subsea fibre to autonomous response, operating entirely on air-gapped edge compute with zero cloud dependencies.

Layer 1 — Subsea
Coherent Optical Sensing — Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) at 10 kHz per strand and State-of-Polarization (SOP) monitoring extract raw phase & polarization telemetry from active trans-oceanic trunks without interrupting data traffic.
▼
Layer 2 — Edge
Zero-Copy SIMD Classification — Mojo AVX-512 vectorized kernels ingest raw optical streams via Zig PCIe DMA drivers at O(1) complexity, classifying seismic, maritime, and kinetic threat signatures in <0.12ms per frame.
▼
Layer 3 — PQC Tunnel
Post-Quantum Encrypted Command Channel — All inter-terminal telemetry and threat alerts are transmitted through ML-KEM-1024 encrypted tunnels with QKD-derived session keys, defeating harvest-now-decrypt-later adversaries.
▼
Layer 4 — Sentinel
Autonomous Threat Response — Rust/eBPF kernel-level apoptosis hooks isolate compromised trunk segments in <1ms, revoke PQC tunnel keys, and reroute traffic to alternate defence paths — fully autonomous, zero human latency.
